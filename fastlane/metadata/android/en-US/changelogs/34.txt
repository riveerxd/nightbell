Monitors can be grouped now, and a group is one card with one verdict.

"Is Nightbell up" was never a question about the website or about the repository.
It was a question about both, and the dashboard could only answer it one row at a
time, with you doing the and-ing. A group answers it directly: All 2 operational,
or 1 of 2 is down, in the line where a monitor card puts its host. The worst
member decides it. Pausing one member never decides it unless every member is
paused, because a pause you set last week must not be able to hide an outage.

Long-press monitors and pick a group, or make a new one. A group opens and shuts
with a tap, and its members are still ordinary cards inside it: same long-press,
same re-check, same drag handle. Grouping changes nothing about what gets checked
or what gets announced.

The group's icon comes from a row of previews rather than a text field. Each
member site is offered once, and you can upload your own picture instead, which
travels in the backup rather than sitting in a file the next phone would not have.
A photo picked from the camera roll now arrives the right way up.

Settings is four tabs: Alerts, Checks, Look, About. Twenty cards on one scroll was
a screen nobody reached the bottom of. Each tab remembers where you scrolled it to
and you can swipe between them.

Two smaller things that were wrong. Tapping outside a text field now unfocuses it
and puts the keyboard away. And a section heading in Settings used to sit further
from its own rows than two categories sat from each other, which is fixed
everywhere.

Still no account, no server and nothing leaving the phone except the checks you
asked for.
