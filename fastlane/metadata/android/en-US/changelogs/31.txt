A repository monitor's own screen no longer charts how fast GitHub answered.
3.2.0 took that off the dashboard card and put the star count, the open issue
count and the latest tag there instead, but left a full Response time card
standing one screen further in, plotting the same round trip under a bigger
heading. It is gone from both places now.

Every other kind of monitor keeps its response-time chart, because for those it
is the measurement the monitor exists to make. Nothing else changed.
