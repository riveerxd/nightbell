Nightbell can now watch a mailbox. Give it an email address and a password and it tells you when new mail arrives, with a louder alert for the senders or subjects you name, so a server that only reports problems by email still gets through to your phone. Setup can look up the mail server from the address. A mail server that stops answering is an outage like any other.

It speaks IMAP over TLS or STARTTLS and works with your own server behind Roundcube, the usual hosting panels, and Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo or Fastmail with an app password. Outlook, Proton and Tuta cannot be read this way, and setup says so. The folder can be picked from the server's list, nothing is ever marked read, and the password stays encrypted on the phone, never in a backup. A refused password is tried once and not again until you change it, so it does not keep knocking on a server that bans repeated failures.
