Nightbell can now watch a host as well as a service. A ping monitor sends ICMP echo to an IPv4 or IPv6 address or a name, no root needed, and reports latency and packet loss. A TCP port monitor connects to one port and hangs up, for databases, SSH or a VPN endpoint. Put both on one host and its screen tells you whether the network or the service is the problem.

Any monitor can now wait for several passing checks before it calls an outage over, so a flapping host stops paging, clearing and paging again. Ping and port monitors also list their recent outages and how long each lasted.
