Pulse — uptime monitoring that lives on your phone

Thirty marks for an app whose job is to interrupt you.

Every direction ships as two files: a square plate for the launcher, and a landscape lockup with the wordmark. Each is shown at four sizes, because a monitoring icon that only works at 192 px is one nobody will pick out of a home screen at 3am.

The two-colour rule

The palette comes from the app itself. Signal green is everything working. Alarm red appears exactly once per mark, on the single element that stands for failure. Nothing else gets colour.

That one constraint is what keeps thirty very different shapes recognisably related, and it means every mark carries the product's actual idea: a steady state, and the moment it breaks.

Plates are near-black rather than white so the marks sit correctly on a dark launcher and inside the app's own dark UI.

  • Ink#0B0E13plate, ground
  • Bone#F2F5F8glyph, wordmark
  • Signal#2FD98Aup, healthy, checking
  • Alarm#FF4D57down — once per mark
  • Line#1E2530rules, dividers

Notes on the files

Plate for the launcher, bare mark for the lockup

The square files are full-bleed 512 px plates — that is what an app icon is. The landscape files drop the plate so the mark sits at brand scale beside the wordmark, which is how a lockup behaves in a README header or a settings screen. Direction 04 is the exception: the plate is the mark, so it keeps its container in both. Every lockup mark is fitted from its real ink bounds, so all thirty sit at the same optical weight next to the type.

Wordmark type is not outlined yet

Each lockup names its intended face and falls back to a system sans, serif or mono. Before a chosen direction goes anywhere final, convert the wordmark to paths so it renders identically everywhere. Roughly two thirds want a tight geometric sans; 06, 19 and 26 want an old-style serif; 08, 09, 12, 14, 16, 18, 22, 24 and 28 want a mono.

Android adaptive icons

For an adaptive icon the foreground layer has to survive a circular mask, so any mark that runs close to the plate edge — 02, 03, 08, 10, 12, 14, 15, 16, 21, 28 and 29 — needs roughly 15% inset before it goes into ic_launcher_foreground. 04 already reads correctly under any mask shape.

Regenerating

The thirty icon SVGs are the only hand-authored files. Lockups, PNG exports and this page all derive from them.

python3 docs/brand/build.py --png

A light-ground variant is a two-value swap: plate #0B0E13 → #F2F5F8, glyph #F2F5F8 → #0B0E13. Green and red are legible on both grounds and stay exactly as they are.
